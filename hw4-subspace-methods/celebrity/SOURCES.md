# Celebrity images

Ten photos of Barack Obama from Wikimedia Commons, the extra subject the assignment asks for. In the augmented data set he is subject 418.

Six are public domain as works of the US federal government. The other four are under Creative Commons licenses that require attribution, given here: three CC BY 2.0 and one CC BY-SA 3.0, `obama_04.jpg`. CC BY-SA also requires that adaptations, including the 20 by 20 crop made from it, be shared under the same license.

Some files are the reduced size versions Commons serves rather than the originals.
